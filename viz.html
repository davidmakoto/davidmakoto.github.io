Model: each eccentric raises its corner by a cam offset of E·(1 − cos θ) with E ≈ 0.5 mm of throw. Sensitivity (µm per 5° turn) scales with |sin θ|, so control is fine near 0° and 180° and touchiest near 90°. Plate height is fit as a tilted plane through the two fixed left pivots and the two right cams. A simplified visualization, not a substitute for a real bed mesh.
