Menstruation Station

Secure menstruation cycle tracking with analytics and data that never leaves your phone.

Overview

Menstruation Station is an iOS cycle tracker built on a simple premise: the most sensitive data an app can hold about you should never leave the device that collects it. Everything is stored and processed on the phone.

The tone is deliberately warm rather than clinical — a subject people are often taught to be quiet about gets a bright, unembarrassed interface.

What it does

  • Cycle tracking. Log a period in a couple of taps, without wading through a form.
  • Analytics. Trends and patterns across your own history, so a conversation with a doctor starts from real data.
  • On-device by default. Your data never leaves your phone — nothing to sell, share, or breach.

How it's built

A native iOS app with local storage and on-device analytics, which keeps the privacy guarantee structural rather than a policy promise: there is no server holding cycle data because there is no server in the loop.
