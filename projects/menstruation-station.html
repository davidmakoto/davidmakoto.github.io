Menstruation Station

Cycle tracking and phase-by-phase health guidance, with data that never leaves the device.

Overview

Menstruation Station is a privacy-focused React Native app for menstrual cycle tracking, designed, built, and shipped solo to the iOS App Store. It has sustained 100+ downloads a month since launch with no paid marketing.

The premise is that the most sensitive data an app can hold about you should never leave the device that collects it — so nothing is uploaded, and deleting everything is always one tap away. The tone is deliberately warm rather than clinical: a subject people are often taught to be quiet about gets a bright, unembarrassed interface.

What it does

  • Cycle logging. Track period dates and symptoms in a simple mobile interface.
  • Predictive analytics. Estimates your next cycle from your own logged history.
  • Cycle-phase health guidance. Reference material on common symptoms and what to expect through each phase of the cycle.
  • On-device by default. Your data never leaves the device — nothing to sell, share, or breach.
  • One-tap wipe. An easy-to-reach delete button clears every record on the device at once.

How it's built

A React Native app with a Jest-tested codebase, independently developed and published end to end. Records live in on-device storage, which keeps the privacy guarantee structural rather than a policy promise: there is no server holding cycle data because there is no server in the loop.

Type iOS health app
Role Solo — design, build, and release
Stack React Native, Jest, on-device storage
