Booket CRM

A customer relationship manager focused on bookings — pipeline, scheduling, and client history in one place.

Overview

Most general-purpose CRMs treat a booking as an afterthought bolted onto a sales pipeline. Booket starts from the booking itself: who the client is, what they booked, when it happens, and what has been agreed along the way.

That means the day-to-day view is a calendar and a pipeline at the same time, and a client's full history — past bookings, conversations, and notes — sits one click away instead of scattered across email threads and spreadsheets.

What it does

  • Booking-first pipeline. Track enquiries through to confirmed bookings without forcing them into a generic sales funnel.
  • Scheduling. See what is booked and when, alongside the deals still in progress.
  • Client history. Past bookings, notes, and contact details kept together on one record.
  • Built for small teams. Fast to set up, with no lengthy configuration before it becomes useful.

How it's built

A full-stack web application: a server-rendered and API-backed data model for clients and bookings, with a front end tuned for quick day-to-day data entry rather than occasional deep-dive reporting.
