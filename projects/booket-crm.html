Booket CRM

A TCPA-compliant SMS marketing platform for small businesses, with LLM-assisted message generation.

Overview

Booket CRM is a TCPA-compliant SMS marketing platform for small businesses, live on the iOS App Store with paying customers. It layers LLM-assisted message generation on top of standard campaign and billing tooling.

What it does

  • Compliant opt-in forms. Auto-generates TCPA-compliant sign-up forms with a business's own branding and a QR code link.
  • AI message drafting. Routes business context through Claude, OpenRouter, and Together.ai to draft campaign messages and suggest tags.
  • Overnight precomputation. A nightly batch job precomputes tags and candidate messages on low-cost models, cutting per-request latency and cost.
  • Smart send timing. Flags low-volume windows and auto-generates promo codes to get a campaign live in a few taps.
  • Built-in billing. Stripe and Apple in-app purchases handle subscriptions, Twilio handles delivery, and Sentry watches production.

How it's built

A Java/Spring Boot backend on PostgreSQL orchestrates campaign workflows, billing, and the LLM routing layer behind a mobile-first dashboard.

Type SaaS product
