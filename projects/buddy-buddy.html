Buddy Buddy

A social matching app for pairing people up around shared goals, habits, and activities.

Overview

Buddy Buddy pairs people who want to do the same thing — train for something, build a habit, pick up a hobby — on the premise that the single biggest predictor of sticking with a goal is having somebody else expecting you to show up.

Rather than a feed to scroll, the app is built around the match itself: find someone with a compatible goal and schedule, agree on the commitment, and keep each other honest.

What it does

  • Goal-based matching. Paired on what you actually want to do, not just proximity.
  • Shared accountability. The pairing is the product — both sides commit to the same thing.
  • Habits and activities. Works for training, studying, hobbies, or any recurring commitment.
  • Lightweight by design. No endless feed; the app gets out of the way once you are matched.

How it's built

Built as a web app with a matching layer over user goals, availability, and activity preferences, kept deliberately simple so the matching logic stays easy to reason about and tune.
